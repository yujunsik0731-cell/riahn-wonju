Perms & Straightening

RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Dangu Lotte Cinema/Perms & Straightening

It starts with hair diagnosis —
perms in Dangu-dong

RIAHN Hair Dangu Lotte Cinema uses Supraliss damage-care products and a low-damage perm process based on a diagnosis of your hair.

Each designer has a specialty: iron perms and men's design perms (Taemin), men's volume straightening and spin swallow perms (Geonu), layered, build and hippie perms (Dami), men's see-through shadow and As perms (Gyuri), and repair straightening for very curly hair (Iji). For aftercare, see our treatments and scalp care too.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting an iron perm that looks blow-dried just by drying
  • Men looking for a shadow, As or see-through perm
  • Those wanting a layered perm that suits their face
  • Very curly hair — considering repair straightening

FAQ

Perm FAQ

How much is a men's perm?
Men's shadow, parted/As and see-through/leaf perms are ₩80,000 each. A down perm is ₩20,000 and a full down perm ₩80,000.
Who should I see for an iron perm?
Taemin specializes in iron perms that keep a blow-dried look when you simply dry your hair after shampooing. An iron perm is ₩150,000.
Can you straighten very curly hair?
Store Manager Iji specializes in straightening very curly hair and repair straightening. We start with a hair diagnosis and consultation.
