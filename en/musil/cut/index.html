RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Musil/Haircuts

Cuts that consider your head shape —
haircuts in Musil-dong

The seven designers at RIAHN Hair Musil build on precise cutting to suggest designs tailored to your head shape and face.

Each designer has a specialty, from men's leaf, two-block and see-through dandy cuts to women's high-layered and hush cuts. For volume or waves, see our perms and volume straightening too.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting a face-slimming high-layered or layered cut
  • Men looking for a leaf, two-block or dandy cut
  • Those who just want a quick bangs or side-bangs trim
  • Anyone who wants a cut that is easy to style in the morning

FAQ

Haircut FAQ

How much is a haircut?
A men's haircut (shampoo included) is ₩18,000 and a women's haircut is ₩23,000. Bangs trims and side bangs are ₩5,000 each.
Do you have men's haircut specialists?
Aon and Yui specialize in leaf, two-block and see-through cuts, Chaerin in see-through dandy cuts, and Kang Suhyun in dandy and pillers cuts.
Who should I see for a layered cut?
Store Manager Gunju specializes in high-layered cuts, and Subin in layered and hush cuts.
