Perms & Straightening

Layered perms, men's perms and volume straightening, with a step-by-step perm process after hair analysis.

RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Dangwan/Perms & Straightening

It starts with hair analysis —
perms in Dangu-dong

RIAHN Hair Dangwan uses Botox and Supraliss damage-care products in every service, with a step-by-step perm process planned after analysing your hair.

Each designer has a specialty — layered, S-curl and C-curl perms, men's perms such as shadow and parted perms, or repair straightening for very curly hair. For aftercare, see our treatments and scalp care too.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting a layered S-curl, C-curl or hippie perm
  • Men looking for a shadow, parted or As perm
  • Very curly hair — considering volume or repair straightening
  • Those worried about heat perms on damaged hair

Perm FAQ

How much is a men's perm?
Shadow, parted/As and see-through/leaf perms are ₩80,000 each. A down perm is ₩20,000 and a full down perm ₩80,000.
How do I get a designer event package?
Designer event packages are priced from the Naver Booking "Event" menu and may change depending on the period and conditions. Choose the package on Naver Booking.
Can you straighten very curly hair?
Store Manager Yunmi specializes in very curly hair, repair perms and repair straightening. We check your hair condition during the consultation first.
