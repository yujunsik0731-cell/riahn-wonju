Store Manager

Iji Store Manager

Off: Tue, Wed
Straightening very curly hair · Repair straightening care · Layered perm · CS-curl perm · Hush cut

Store manager specializing in repair straightening for very curly hair and repair services for damaged hair.

[Hair science & principles] A mechanism specialist who redesigns the structure of curls. I'm Iji, a 'chemical master' who understands the bonds in hair. Anyone can simply straighten hair with a flat iron. But stably rearranging the irregular S-S (disulfide) bonds of very curly hair and preventing protein loss inside the hair takes a skilled expert. I don't do services that only make the surface smooth with silicone. I aim for repair services that fill the CMC (cell membrane complex) inside the hair so it becomes stronger from within. I'll give you my best style, aiming for your satisfaction and delight.
