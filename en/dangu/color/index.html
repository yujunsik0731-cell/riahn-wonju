Hair Color & Bleach

Low-damage bleaching and skin-tone-flattering color suggested after a 1:1 personal color analysis.

RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Dangu Lotte Cinema/Hair Color

Matched to your skin tone and eyes —
hair color in Dangu-dong

Ain at RIAHN Hair Dangu Lotte Cinema suggests a personal recipe matched to your skin tone and eye color rather than whatever is trending, with a careful consultation including 1:1 personal color analysis, low-damage bleaching and skin-tone-flattering color.

Women's color has fixed prices by length, and there are root touch-up and root bleach menus with or without a cut, plus men's color and bleach. Balayage and ombré are done after a consultation. For care after coloring, see our treatments and scalp care.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants to find their best color with a personal color analysis
  • Those worried about damage but ready to try bleach
  • Anyone interested in balayage or ombré
  • Men looking for color or bleach

FAQ

Hair Color FAQ

How much is a root touch-up?
Root touch-up (cut not included) is ₩60,000, or ₩73,000 with a cut. Root bleach (cut not included) is ₩80,000, or ₩103,000 with a cut.
How much is men's color?
Men's color is ₩60,000, men's color & cut ₩68,000 and men's bleach (cut not included) ₩70,000.
Do you offer personal color analysis?
Ain gives a 1:1 personal color analysis and then suggests a low-damage bleach and color recipe matched to your skin tone and eye color.
