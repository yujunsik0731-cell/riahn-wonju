FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about booking, discounts, parking, opening hours and prices.

RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Dangwan/FAQ
How do I book?
Book a date and time on Naver Booking. You can also call us (+82-33-762-7525) or send an Instagram DM (@riahnhair_danguan).
Is there a Naver Booking discount?
Yes, 20% off when you book via Naver. Cuts, blow-dries and partial services are excluded.
Where is the salon?
1F, 389 Dangu-ro, Wonju-si, Gangwon-do (강원 원주시 단구로 389, 1층). From the Dangu-dong Outlet intersection, head toward Dangwan Taekji; it's about 50 m before the second roundabout, across from Paris Baguette.
Is there parking?
Yes, free parking is available.
What are your opening hours?
We're open every day, 10:00–20:00.
How much do services cost?
Haircuts ₩5,000–₩23,000, perms and straightening ₩20,000–₩290,000, color ₩60,000–₩250,000, treatments and scalp care ₩30,000–₩250,000. Prices are fixed by hair length.
Will there be extra charges?
We charge the prices on our menu. If any additional charge applies, we always explain it before the service.
What happens on my first visit?
During the consultation we look at your face shape, hair type and condition, and lifestyle, then suggest a design that is easy to look after.
Can I request a specific designer?
Please choose your preferred designer when booking. See our Designers page for each designer's specialty.
What are designer event packages?
Perm and straightening packages by Yuhyeon, Jia, Jenny, Yunmi and Anne at Naver Booking event prices, which may change depending on the period and conditions.

Still have questions? Call us during opening hours (every day 10:00–20:00).
