FAQ

Careers FAQ

How do I apply?
Apply by text or phone, online (HairInJob), by walking in, or by visiting after a call. The hiring contact is Director Kwon Wiho (010-3122-9770).
What documents do I need?
Please bring a short résumé and profile.
What are the working hours?
Working hours are 10:00–20:00. Designers work 6 days a week (alternate-week 5-day or 5-day week negotiable); hair staff work 5 days a week.
What training is available?
You can choose from the RIAHN hair academy's 5-level job training, overseas technical training, influencer trend classes, and Naver and social-media marketing training.
