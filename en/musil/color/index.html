Hair Color & Bleach

Low-damage bleaching and color suggested after a 1:1 personal color analysis.

RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Musil/Hair Color

Matched to your skin tone and eyes —
hair color in Musil-dong

RIAHN Hair Musil suggests low-damage bleaching and color based on a 1:1 personal color analysis. Director Yeongseok, our personal-color specialist, suggests recipes matched to your skin tone and eye color.

Women's color has fixed prices by length, and there are root touch-up and root bleach menus with or without a cut, plus men's color and bleach. For care after coloring, see our treatments and scalp care.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants to find their best color with a personal color analysis
  • Those worried about damage but ready to try bleach
  • Those who just want to touch up their roots
  • Men looking for color or bleach

Hair Color FAQ

How much is a root touch-up?
Root touch-up is ₩60,000, or ₩73,000 with a cut. Root bleach is ₩80,000, or ₩103,000 with a cut.
How much is men's color?
Men's color is ₩60,000, color & cut ₩68,000 and men's bleach ₩70,000.
Do you offer personal color analysis?
Director Yeongseok gives a 1:1 personal color analysis and then suggests a low-damage bleach and color recipe matched to your skin tone and eye color.
