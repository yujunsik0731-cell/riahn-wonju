About

We look at your face shape, hair
and lifestyle — RIAHN Hair Dangwan

RIAHN Hair Dangwan is at 1F, 389 Dangu-ro, Wonju, Gangwon-do — toward Dangwan Taekji from the Dangu-dong Outlet intersection, close to Dangwan Taekji, Gwanseol-dong and Wonju Innovation City.

Open since 2019 and rated a top salon in RIAHN Hair's national evaluation in 2023, 2024 and 2025, our six designers offer layered, S-curl and C-curl perms, men's perms such as shadow and parted perms, repair straightening for very curly hair, bleaching and color.

During the consultation we look at your face shape, hair type and condition, and lifestyle, and suggest a design that works now and is easy to look after later.

Why RIAHN Hair Dangwan

  • Tailored designs built on precise cutting, using graduation and layering techniques
  • Botox and Supraliss damage-care products used in every service, with a step-by-step perm process after hair analysis
  • 20% off when you book via Naver (excluding cuts, blow-dries and partial services)
  • Free parking · 10 styling chairs · Open every day 10:00–20:00
