FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about booking, parking, opening hours, prices and designers.

RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Musil/FAQ
How do I book?
Choose a date, time and designer on Naver Booking. You can also call us (+82-33-743-7525).
Where is the salon?
1F Young Villa, 1 Bongbawi-gil, Wonju-si, Gangwon-do (강원 원주시 봉바위길 1 영빌라 1층), in Musil-dong.
Is there parking?
You can use the RIAHN Hair parking lot (Suki-ne Pojangmacha parking lot in Musil-dong, free) and the Musam Park parking lot (2 hours free; free on weekends and public holidays).
What are your opening hours?
We're open every day, 10:00–20:00.
How much do services cost?
Haircuts ₩5,000–₩23,000, perms and straightening ₩20,000–₩290,000, color ₩60,000–₩250,000, treatments and scalp care ₩30,000–₩250,000. Prices are fixed by hair length.
Will there be extra charges?
We charge the prices on our menu. If any additional charge applies, we always explain it before the service.
What happens on my first visit?
Your designer looks at your head shape, face and hair type and suggests a tailored design. Perms follow a step-by-step process after hair analysis to keep damage to a minimum.
Can I request a specific designer?
Please choose your preferred designer when booking. See our Designers page for each designer's specialty.
What are designer event packages?
Perm and color packages by Gunju, Yeongseok, Kang Suhyun, Subin, Chaerin, Yui and Aon at Naver Booking event prices. You pay for the service and amount confirmed after a consultation at the salon, and prices may change depending on the period and conditions.
Do you offer personal color analysis?
Director Yeongseok gives a 1:1 personal color analysis and suggests a low-damage bleach and color recipe matched to your skin tone and eye color.

Still have questions? Call us during opening hours (every day 10:00–20:00).
