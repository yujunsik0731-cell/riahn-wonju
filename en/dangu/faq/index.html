FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about booking, parking, opening hours, prices and designers.

RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Dangu Lotte Cinema/FAQ
How do I book?
Choose a date, time and designer on Naver Booking. You can also call us (+82-33-763-7525) or send an Instagram DM.
Where is the salon?
1F, 405 Seowon-daero, Wonju-si, Gangwon-do (강원 원주시 서원대로 405 1층), near Lotte Cinema in Dangu-dong.
Is there parking?
If you park at the GS parking lot (403-14 Seowon-daero), we provide parking support depending on your service.
What are your opening hours?
We're open every day, 10:00–20:00. Please check each designer's days off on our Designers page.
How much do services cost?
Haircuts ₩5,000–₩23,000, perms and straightening ₩20,000–₩290,000, color ₩60,000–₩250,000, treatments and scalp care ₩30,000–₩250,000. Prices are fixed by hair length, and any additional charge is explained before the service.
What happens on my first visit?
Your designer consults with you 1:1, looks at your hair condition, face shape and lifestyle, and suggests a tailored design.
Can I request a specific designer?
Please choose your preferred designer when booking. See our Designers page for each designer's specialty and days off.
Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept Apple Pay, local currency (paper, card and mobile), Zero Pay and mobile payments.
Can I bring my pet?
Yes, pets are welcome.
Are there any discounts?
20% off services when you book via Naver (excluding cuts and blow-dries)
Can you work with very curly or damaged hair?
Store Manager Iji specializes in repair straightening for very curly hair and repair services for damaged hair. We use Supraliss damage-care products and a low-damage process based on hair diagnosis.

Still have questions? Call us during opening hours (every day 10:00–20:00).
