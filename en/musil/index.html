About

Designs tailored to your head shape
and face — RIAHN Hair Musil

RIAHN Hair Musil is on the ground floor at 1 Bongbawi-gil (Musil-dong), Wonju, Gangwon-do, and has been open in Musil-dong since 2013.

Our six designers build on precise cutting to suggest designs that suit your head shape and face, offering trend perms such as layered, repair, down and see-through perms, plus color and bleaching based on personal color analysis. We also offer hair spa and scalp care.

Free RIAHN Hair parking (Suki-ne Pojangmacha parking lot in Musil-dong) and the Musam Park parking lot (2 hours free; free on weekends and public holidays) are available. 6 styling chairs, open every day 10:00–20:00.

Why RIAHN Hair Musil

  • Six designers, each dedicated to their field — men's perms (see-through, leaf, As, shadow) and women's layered and repair perms
  • Low-damage bleaching and color suggested after a 1:1 personal color analysis
  • Botox and Supraliss damage-care products used in every service, with a step-by-step perm process after hair analysis
  • Hair spa and scalp care
  • Free RIAHN Hair parking
  • 20% off services when you book via Naver (excluding cuts, blow-dries and partial services)
  • Salon with the most Naver bookings in 2025
  • Chosen by customers as an outstanding Naver Booking salon
