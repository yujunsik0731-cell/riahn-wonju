Hair Color & Bleach

Trend colors that flatter your skin tone, plus personal color consulting to find your shade.

RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Gieopdosi/Hair Color

Colors that flatter your skin tone —
hair color in Wonju Enterprise City

RIAHN Hair Gieopdosi offers trend colors such as ash brown, ash beige and mocha mousse, as well as bleaching. Director Jeongjin creates trendy colors that flatter your skin tone, Ihyeon designs based on personal color and image consulting, and Hyomin specializes in vivid red colors.

Women's color has fixed prices by length, and there are root touch-up and root bleach menus with or without a cut, plus men's color and bleach. For care after coloring, see our treatments and scalp care.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting trend colors like ash brown or ash beige
  • Those who want a shade matched to their personal color
  • Those who just want to touch up their roots
  • Men looking for color or bleach

FAQ

Hair Color FAQ

How much is a root touch-up?
Women's root touch-up (cut not included) is ₩60,000, or ₩73,000 with a cut. Root bleach (cut not included) is ₩80,000, or ₩103,000 with a cut.
How much is men's color?
Men's color is ₩60,000, color & cut ₩68,000 and men's bleach (cut not included) ₩70,000.
Do you offer personal color consulting?
Ihyeon suggests styles that bring out your best, based on personal color and image consulting.
