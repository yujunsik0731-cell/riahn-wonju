RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Gieopdosi/Treatment & Scalp Care

Smooth texture before and after perms or color —
treatments in Wonju Enterprise City

RIAHN Hair Gieopdosi offers treatments by length and men's and women's scalp care. Olaplex and Recharge treatments are add-ons with a perm or color.

Perm & treatment and color & treatment are 30% off. Hyomin specializes in tailored treatment care. If you're getting a perm, see our perms and straightening too.

Recommended for

  • Hair that feels rough after perms or color
  • Anyone wanting Olaplex or Recharge care with a perm or color
  • Those who want scalp care as well
  • Men wanting a cut and scalp care in one visit

Treatment FAQ

How much is a treatment?
Treatments by length are Short ₩100,000, Medium ₩150,000, Long ₩200,000 and Long Long ₩250,000. Olaplex treatment is ₩30,000 and Recharge treatment ₩50,000.
Can I get an Olaplex treatment on its own?
Olaplex and Recharge treatments are add-ons with a perm or color and are not available on their own.
Do you offer scalp care?
Yes — men's scalp care ₩60,000, men's scalp care & cut ₩68,000, and women's scalp care (cut not included) ₩80,000.
