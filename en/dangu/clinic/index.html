Smooth texture before and after perms or color —
treatments in Dangu-dong

RIAHN Hair Dangu Lotte Cinema uses Supraliss damage-care products and checks your hair condition during the consultation before suggesting a service.

Choose from treatments by length, Olaplex and Recharge treatments, and men's and women's scalp care. If you're getting a perm, see our perms and straightening too.

Recommended for

  • Hair that feels rough after perms or color
  • Anyone needing care after bleaching
  • Those who want scalp care as well
  • Men wanting a cut and scalp care in one visit

Treatment FAQ

How much is a treatment?
Treatments are priced by length, from ₩100,000 for Short to ₩250,000 for Long Long. Olaplex treatment is ₩30,000 and Recharge treatment ₩50,000.
Do you offer scalp care?
Yes — men's scalp care ₩60,000, men's scalp care & cut ₩68,000, and women's scalp care (cut not included) ₩80,000.
Who should I see for damaged-hair repair?
Store Manager Iji specializes in repair straightening for very curly hair and repair services for damaged hair.
