About

Designs matched to your hair, face shape
and lifestyle — RIAHN Hair Dangu Lotte Cinema

RIAHN Hair Dangu Lotte Cinema is on the ground floor at 405 Seowon-daero, Wonju, near Lotte Cinema in Dangu-dong. On Naver it ranks first in Wonju for visits by customer recommendation and referral and for repeat visits, with more than 2,000 satisfied-customer reviews and more than 2,000 trend perm and trend color services a month on average.

Open since 2019 with 8 styling chairs, we offer everything from cuts, perms and color to scalp care. Our six designers — specializing in personal color and low-damage bleaching, repair straightening for very curly hair, high-layered cuts and layered perms, iron perms, and men's shadow and As perms — consult with you 1:1 and suggest a design that suits your hair condition, face shape and lifestyle.

Free parking is available at the GS parking lot, 403-14 Seowon-daero. We're open every day 10:00–20:00 and take Naver bookings. Pets are welcome, and we accept Apple Pay, local currency (paper, card and mobile), Zero Pay and mobile payments.

Why RIAHN Hair Dangu Lotte Cinema

  • Six designers, each with a specialty: iron perms and men's design perms (Taemin), personal color and low-damage bleaching (Ain), men's volume straightening and barber design cuts (Geonu), high-layered cuts and layered perms (Dami), men's see-through shadow and As perms (Gyuri), very curly hair and repair straightening (Iji)
  • On Naver: No. 1 in Wonju for recommended and referred visits and for repeat visits, with 2,000+ satisfied reviews a month on average
  • Supraliss damage-care products and a low-damage perm process based on hair diagnosis
  • Fixed prices by hair length; any extra charge is explained before the service
  • Open every day, free parking, pets welcome
  • 20% off services when you book via Naver (excluding cuts and blow-dries)
