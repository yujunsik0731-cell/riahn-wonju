About

We check your hair and face shape first —
RIAHN Hair Gieopdosi

RIAHN Hair Gieopdosi is on the 3rd floor of the Jijeong Galbi building (Star Edu Building) at 20 Dowon-ro, Jijeong-myeon, Wonju, diagonally across from Starbucks in Wonju Enterprise City — the largest hair salon in Wonju.

In the most spacious salon in Wonju, with 12 comfortable styling chairs, designers selected as outstanding by the Korea Consumer Industry Evaluation offer their signature menus: layered cuts, hippie perms, volume straightening, iron perms, repair perms, trend colors, men's and kids' cuts, elegant styles for middle-aged guests and hair for the parents of the bride and groom.

We first check your hair condition and face shape, then suggest a tailored design that is easy to style whatever your hair condition. The building's basement car park is free during your service, and we're open every day 10:30–20:00 with Naver Booking.

Why RIAHN Hair Gieopdosi

  • Six specialist designers: men's & kids' cuts (Juhee), repair perms & trend colors (Jeongjin), build & hippie perms (Hyomin), iron perms & volume straightening (Iseul), bobs, layered cuts & personal color (Ihyeon), very curly hair & repair straightening (Hyeonyeong)
  • Supraliss damage-care products, with a step-by-step perm process after hair analysis to keep damage to a minimum
  • Precise tailored cuts using gradation and layering techniques
  • Fixed prices by hair length; any additional charge explained before the service
  • Wonju's largest salon with 12 chairs, free parking, open every day
