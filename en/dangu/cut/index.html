RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Dangu Lotte Cinema/Haircuts

Cuts matched to your face and lifestyle —
haircuts in Dangu-dong

The six designers at RIAHN Hair Dangu Lotte Cinema consult with you 1:1 on your hair condition, face shape and lifestyle before suggesting a design.

Each has a specialty: high-layered cuts matched to cheekbones and jawline (Dami), barber design cuts based on facial proportions (Geonu), layered cuts (Taemin), tassel cuts (Ain), hush cuts (Iji) and men's cuts (Gyuri). For volume or waves, see our perms and volume straightening too.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting a face-slimming high-layered cut
  • Men looking for a barber design cut matched to their proportions
  • Those who just want a quick bangs or side-bangs trim
  • Anyone who wants a cut that is easy to style in the morning

FAQ

Haircut FAQ

How much is a haircut?
A men's haircut (shampoo included) is ₩18,000 and a women's haircut is ₩23,000. Bangs trims and side bangs are ₩5,000 each.
Who should I see for a high-layered cut?
Dami specializes in high-layered cuts with custom layering matched to the cheekbones, jawline and neckline.
Do you have men's haircut specialists?
Geonu specializes in barber design cuts based on facial proportion analysis, and Gyuri in men's cuts.
