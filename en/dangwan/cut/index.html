RIAHN Hair Dangwan builds tailored designs on precise cutting, using graduation and layering techniques.

During the consultation we look at your face shape, hair type and condition, and lifestyle, and suggest a cut that is easy to look after. For waves or volume, see our perms and volume straightening too.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants a layered cut that suits their face shape
  • Men looking for a haircut with shampoo included
  • Those who just want a quick bangs or side-bangs trim
  • Anyone near Dangwan Taekji or Gwanseol-dong who wants an easy-care cut

FAQ

Haircut FAQ

How much is a haircut?
A men's haircut (shampoo included) is ₩18,000 and a women's haircut is ₩23,000. Bangs trims and side bangs are ₩5,000 each.
Does the Naver Booking discount apply to haircuts?
The 20% Naver Booking discount applies to services other than cuts, blow-dries and partial services, so haircuts are not discounted.
Is there a consultation before the cut?
Yes. We look at your face shape, hair type and condition, and lifestyle, and suggest a design that is easy to look after.
