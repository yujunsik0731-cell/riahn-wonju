Treatment FAQ

How much is a treatment?
Treatments by length are Short ₩100,000, Medium ₩150,000, Long ₩200,000 and Long Long ₩250,000. Olaplex treatment is ₩30,000 and Recharge treatment ₩50,000.
Do you offer scalp care?
Yes — men's scalp care ₩60,000, men's scalp care & cut ₩68,000, and women's scalp care ₩80,000.
Can I get color and scalp care together?
Subin's 'Bliss' Duo Package combines personal color analysis, low-damage color and a design cut with scalp cleansing and soothing, nourishing scalp care. See the designer event packages on our Hair Color page for prices.
