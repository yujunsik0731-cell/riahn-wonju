Hair Color & Bleach

From bleaching and trend colors to root touch-ups and men's color — fixed prices by hair length.

RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Dangwan/Hair Color

Color matched to your face and mood —
hair color in Dangu-dong

Designer Aran suggests styles matched to your face shape and mood, building on bleaching, trend colors and layered perms.

Women's color has fixed prices by length, and there are root touch-up and root bleach menus, with or without a cut. For care after coloring, see our treatments and scalp care.

Recommended for

  • Anyone ready to try bleach or a trend color
  • Those who just want to touch up their roots
  • Anyone who wants color and a cut in one visit
  • Men looking for color or bleach

Hair Color FAQ

How much is a root touch-up?
Root touch-up is ₩60,000, or ₩73,000 with a cut. Root bleach is ₩80,000, or ₩103,000 with a cut.
How much is men's color?
Men's color is ₩60,000, color & cut ₩68,000 and men's bleach ₩70,000.
Is there a bleach specialist?
Designer Aran specializes in bleaching, trend colors and layered perms.
