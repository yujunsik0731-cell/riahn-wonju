Perms & Straightening

Volume straightening, iron perms, repair perms and hippie perms, with a step-by-step process after hair analysis that keeps damage to a minimum.

RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Gieopdosi/Perms & Straightening

It starts with hair analysis —
perms in Wonju Enterprise City

RIAHN Hair Gieopdosi uses Supraliss damage-care products and follows a step-by-step perm process after hair analysis to keep damage to a minimum.

Each designer has a specialty: very curly hair and repair straightening (Owner Hyeonyeong), repair perms (Director Jeongjin), iron perms and volume straightening (Iseul), build and hippie perms (Hyomin), and parted, shadow and down perms (Juhee). For aftercare, see our treatments and scalp care too.

Recommended for

  • Very curly hair — considering repair or volume straightening
  • Damaged hair that needs a repair perm
  • Anyone wanting an iron perm that sets just by drying
  • Anyone wanting waves like hippie, build or S-curl perms

FAQ

Perm FAQ

How much are volume straightening and iron perms?
Volume straightening is ₩120,000 and an iron perm ₩150,000. Setting perms and straightening are ₩150,000 to ₩290,000 by length.
How much is a men's perm?
Shadow, parted/As and see-through/leaf perms are ₩80,000 each, and a full down perm (cut not included) is ₩80,000.
Is there a discount for a perm with a treatment?
Yes — 30% off perm & treatment, and 30% off color & treatment.
