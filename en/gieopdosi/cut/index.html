RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Gieopdosi/Haircuts

Gradation and layering —
tailored cuts in Wonju Enterprise City

RIAHN Hair Gieopdosi offers precise tailored cuts using gradation and layering techniques. We check your hair condition and face shape first, then suggest a design that is easy to style.

Each designer has a specialty, from men's and kids' cuts such as junior, dandy and wolf cuts to women's bobs, layered and shaggy cuts. For volume or waves, see our perms and volume straightening too.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting a layered cut or bob that suits their face
  • Men looking for a dandy or wolf cut
  • Parents booking a kids' (junior) cut
  • Anyone preparing for a special day with a volume blow-dry

Haircut FAQ

How much is a haircut?
A men's haircut (shampoo included) is ₩18,000 and a women's haircut is ₩23,000. Bangs trims and side bangs are ₩5,000 each.
Can I book just a cut?
Cut bookings are available only when you pay with Naver Pay. Please pay with Naver Pay on Naver Booking.
Who should I see for men's or kids' cuts?
Juhee specializes in junior, dandy and wolf cuts, and Ihyeon in women's bobs, layered and shaggy cuts.
