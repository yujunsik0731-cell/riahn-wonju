Perms & Straightening

Men's perms, layered perms, repair perms and volume straightening, with a step-by-step process that keeps damage to a minimum.

RIAHN Hair Wonju/RIAHN Hair Musil/Perms & Straightening

It starts with hair analysis —
perms in Musil-dong

RIAHN Hair Musil uses Botox and Supraliss damage-care products in every service, with a step-by-step perm process planned after analysing your hair to keep damage to a minimum.

Our six designers each focus on their field — men's perms (see-through, leaf, As, shadow) or women's layered and repair perms. For aftercare, see our treatments and scalp care too.

Recommended for

  • Men looking for a see-through, leaf, As or shadow perm
  • Anyone wanting a layered, C-curl or jelly perm that suits their face
  • Damaged hair — considering a repair perm or volume straightening
  • Sides that stick out and need a down perm

Perm FAQ

How much is a men's perm?
Shadow, parted/As and see-through/leaf perms are ₩80,000 each. A down perm is ₩20,000.
How do designer event packages work?
Designer event packages are Naver Booking event prices. You pay for the service and amount confirmed after a consultation with your designer at the salon, and prices may change depending on the period and conditions.
Can you perm damaged hair?
Store Manager Gunju and Subin specialize in repair perms. We follow a step-by-step perm process after hair analysis to keep damage to a minimum.
